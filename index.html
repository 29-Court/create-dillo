Anatomy

What is a dillo?

A dillo is a box. Your box. Everything your app needs to exist lives inside it — and every wall of the box can be swapped, expanded, or ripped out. Your call.

Wall one

Files

Your files, with permissions attached. Powered by R2 by default — uploads, storage, and access rules that respect the same permission words as everything else. Your users’ stuff, armored.

Wall two

Database

Your data, validated and permissioned at the schema level. Powered by D1 by default — JSON records with real types flowing straight to your client. Expand it, swap it, grow it. Your call.

Wall three

Functions

Your logic, at the edge. defineFunction() — validated inputs, typed outputs, callable straight from your UI with api.functions.whatever(). Middleware, secrets, and integrations live here, not scattered across provider dashboards.

Roadmap — not shipped
Wall four

Micro-apps

The vision: AI-built apps living on top of your dillo — generated against your schema, permissioned by your backend, deployed without a second thought. This one isn’t built yet. We’re not selling you a drawing of a house.

The deal

Permissions

The backend holds all the permissions. Every record, every file, every function call runs through the armor. You don’t sprinkle auth across seventeen services and pray.

Ownership

Your data is yours. Private by default, on infrastructure you control. No silent exfiltration, no “trust us” clause. If you want to leave, the box comes with you.

Responsibility

Ship the dumb stuff. Break your own data? That’s on you. We’re not your mom. But the backend will never be the thing that breaks first — that part we guarantee with armor.

Frontend

No approval needed. For anything. Build the wildest UI you can imagine — the dillo underneath doesn’t care what it looks like, only that the data contract holds.

The whole thing

Three files.
One backend.

This is real code. Describe what your data looks like, define what your app does, declare where it runs. Done.

schema.ts — what your data looks like. Who can touch it.
import { boolean, owner, schema, string, table } from "create-dillo/schema";

export default schema({
  Project: table({
    name: string().min(1).max(120),
    active: boolean().default(true),
  }).permissions({
    view: owner,   // the armor: enforced on every request
    edit: owner,
  }),
});
backend.ts — what your app does. Functions, validated. 
import { defineBackend, defineFunction } from "create-dillo/backend";

export default defineBackend({
  schema,
  functions: {
    greet: defineFunction()
      .input({ name: string().min(1) })
      .output({ message: string() })
      .handler((_ctx, input) => ({ message: `Hello ${input.name}` })),
  },
});
armadillo.config.ts — where it runs. Your infrastructure.
import { app } from "create-dillo/deploy/cloudflare";
import backend from "./backend.ts";

export default app({ id: "my-app", backend, signup: "open" });
// Workers + D1 + R2, wired. Deploy when you're ready.
