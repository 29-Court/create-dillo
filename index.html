Marietta, Texas — 2015

A man went outside at 3 a.m. with a .38 revolver and fired three times at an armadillo on his property.

At least one bullet ricocheted off the animal’s hard shell and struck the man in the jaw. He was airlifted to a hospital.

The armadillo was never found.

Lee County, Georgia — 2015

A man fired a 9mm at an armadillo. The bullet ricocheted, went through a fence, through the back door of a mobile home, through a recliner — and struck his mother-in-law.

Shoot at the shell. The shell wins.

The pattern
